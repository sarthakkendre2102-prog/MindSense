Privacy & Consent

Your Data, Your Choice.

MindSense is built on transparent, privacy-first principles. Please review how multimodal data is utilized within our academic screening research prototype.

Multimodal Data Processing

Self-reported reflections (text), voice acoustics (audio), and facial expression dynamics (image/video) may be processed by feature extraction models solely to compute experimental wellness screening indicators.

IoT Sensor Readings

Passive readings collected by the paired ESP32 biosensor node (heart rate variability, skin temperature, and sleep cycles) serve as contextual physical baseline signals to enhance multimodal fusion accuracy.

Privacy & Data Security

User samples are anonymized, stored in protected client storage during this prototype demonstration, and are never sold or shared with commercial advertising brokers.

Important Research & Medical Notice

MindSense is an academic monitoring and screening prototype. It does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical evaluations, or emergency healthcare. If you are experiencing distress, consult a certified healthcare professional immediately.
