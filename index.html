Academic Research Project

Engineering Technology for Mental Wellness Screening

MindSense is an academic research demonstration developed by engineering students to explore the intersection of Web Engineering, Machine Learning, and Internet of Things (IoT) hardware.

By fusing unobtrusive wearable IoT signals with self-reported text reflections and voice acoustics, the system demonstrates how pervasive computing can assist in monitoring wellness-related trends.

Ethical & Medical Scope Notice

This system provides screening and monitoring estimates and is not a medical diagnosis or replacement for professional healthcare. Always consult licensed medical practitioners for diagnostic evaluations.
